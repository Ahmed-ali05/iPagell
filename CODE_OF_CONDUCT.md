# Code of conduct

iPagell is used by students, many of them minors. Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, is expected to help keep it welcoming and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Give constructive, specific feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming.
- Protect privacy: never post real grades, diaries, screenshots with personal data, credentials or information that could identify a student.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Sexual content or any content inappropriate for a school audience.
- Publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour, spam or trolling.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations or block participants who do not follow this code. To report a problem privately, email **ipagell.service@gmail.com** with the subject `Conduct`. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies to all project spaces and to anyone representing the project in public.
